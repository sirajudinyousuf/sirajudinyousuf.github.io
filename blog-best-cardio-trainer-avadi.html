Types of Cardio We Train

Strength meets cardio. Compound movements in rapid succession — builds muscle while burning fat.

Heart Rate Zones: Training Smart

Our Avadi cardio coaches use heart rate zone training to maximize results. Each zone has a specific purpose:

Zone% Max HRPurposeExample
Zone 150–60%Recovery, warm-upEasy walk
Zone 260–70%Fat burning, aerobic baseBrisk walk / easy run
Zone 370–80%Aerobic fitness improvementModerate run / cycling
Zone 480–90%Speed & lactate thresholdTempo run / HIIT
Zone 590–100%Max power, peak performanceSprints

Sample Weekly Cardio Programs

BEGINNER

3 Days/Week — Build Your Base

  • Monday: 25-min brisk walk/jog (Zone 2)
  • Wednesday: 20-min cycling + 10-min core
  • Friday: 30-min mixed cardio circuit (low intensity)
  • Goal: Build aerobic base, improve heart health, lose initial weight
INTERMEDIATE

4 Days/Week — Fat Loss Focus

  • Monday: 30-min HIIT (20s on / 10s rest × 15 rounds)
  • Wednesday: 40-min moderate run (Zone 3)
  • Friday: 35-min circuit training (cardio + strength)
  • Saturday: 45-min steady-state cycling (Zone 2)
ADVANCED

5 Days/Week — Athletic Performance

  • Monday: Speed intervals (10 × 400m at Zone 4)
  • Tuesday: 60-min easy run recovery (Zone 2)
  • Thursday: 45-min HIIT metabolic circuit
  • Friday: Tempo run 30 mins (Zone 3–4)
  • Sunday: Long aerobic effort 60–90 mins
