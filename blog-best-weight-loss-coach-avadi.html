Science of Sustainable Weight Loss

Sustainable fat loss happens at 0.5–1 kg per week. Faster than this, and you're losing muscle mass and water — not fat. Our coaches keep you in the optimal zone: a 300–500 calorie daily deficit through the right combination of exercise and nutrition.

We also prioritize strength training because muscle is metabolically active tissue — the more muscle you have, the more calories you burn at rest. That's why our weight loss programs always include resistance training, not just cardio.
